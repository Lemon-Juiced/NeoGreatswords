# Neo Greatswords
Neo Greatswords is a NeoForge mod that brings Greatswords to Minecraft. Spend a few more resources to craft a weapon that is slightly slower than a standard sword, but hits harder and lasts significantly longer.

## Features
- Adds a Greatsword variant for all vanilla sword materials: Wood, Stone, Copper, Iron, Gold, Diamond, and Netherite
- Greatswords deal more damage than their sword counterparts
- Greatswords have 4x the durability of the equivalent sword material
- Greatswords have a slower attack speed, rewarding timing and commitment over spam

## No Dependencies
Neo Greatswords has no required dependencies outside of NeoForge itself.
